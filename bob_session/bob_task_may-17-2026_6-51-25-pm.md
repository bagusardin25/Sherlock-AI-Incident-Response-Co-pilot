231 | ## Design principles
232 | 
233 | - **Banner only for cinematic surfaces**: bare `sherlock`, `sherlock --help`,
234 |   `sherlock resolve`. Auth and admin commands stay clean.
235 | - **Slash commands inside the shell**: `/resolve`, `/fix`, `/auth login` —
236 |   AI-native UX, similar to Claude Code, Codex, or Gemini CLI.
237 | - **Persistent session context**: active incident travels with the prompt;
238 |   `/fix` and `/open` use it automatically.
239 | - **One renderer, two modes**: shell and one-shot share `views.ts` so
240 |   output is consistent for both demos and automation.
241 | - **Reasoning over noise**: `[AGENT]` tags, then Hypothesis / Evidence /
242 |   Confidence — no boxy panels, no emoji clutter.
